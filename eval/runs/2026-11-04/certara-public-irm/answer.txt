**Key procedural rules (IRM 1.11.2.5.2, paragraph 3‑5)**  

1. **Effective‑date timing** – The IRM effective date must be the same as or later than the MT date; enough time must be allowed for M&P processing, especially for filing‑season IRMs (August‑December).  
2. **Wildcard prohibition for specific‑date IRMs** – When an IRM has a specific effective date, wildcard subsection dates may **not** be used; the specific date must appear in both the MT‑effective‑date element and each affected subsection date.  
3. **Date insertion** – When adding or revising information that becomes effective on a set date, insert that date in **both** the MT effective‑date element **and** the affected subsection date(s).  
4. **Example supplied** – The IRM shows a case where the MT date is **December 15, 2025** and the procedures become effective on **01‑01‑2026**; the table lists the MT date, IRM effective date, subsection dates, and revision date accordingly.  
5. **Reference for MT‑date details** – See IRM 1.11.2.6.1, “Required Elements of the Manual Transmittal”.

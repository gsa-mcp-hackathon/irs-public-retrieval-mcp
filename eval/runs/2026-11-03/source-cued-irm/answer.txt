**IRM Date Rules**  
- Heading `1.11.2.5.2 (06-09-2026)` provides IRM effective date `06-09-2026`.  
- Defined IRM date types (quoted table):  
  - `MT date` definition `This is the date M&P publishes the IRM section and posts in the Core Repository of Published Products (housed on the Electronic Publishing website). Refer to IRM 1.11.2.6.1.1, Manual Transmittal (MT) Date.` format `Month DD, YYYY`.  
  - `Effective date` definition `This is the date the new or revised IRM section is effective. The IRM effective date can be: A. The same date as the MT date, or B. A specific future date.` format wildcard `(MM-DD-YYYY)` or specific `(01-01-2026)`.  
  - `Subsection date` definition `This is the effective date of the information in the IRM subsection. The subsection date can be: A. The same date as the revision date, or B. The effective date.` format wildcard `(MM-DD-YYYY)` or specific `(01-01-2026)`.  
  - `Revision date` definition `This date reflects the MT date. The revision date only appears in PDF versions of the IRM and Document 10988, IRM Index.` format PDF `(MM-DD-YYYY)` and Index `MM-YYYY`.  
- Rule 3.A (`Effective date` must be same or later than MT date).  
- Rule 3.B (`Do not use wildcard subsection dates`).  
- Rule 4 (`When adding or revising information that becomes effective on a specified date, insert the date in both the MT effective date element and the affected subsection date(s)`).

**Subsection Date Rules** (`1.11.2.5.2.1` heading `(09-13-2023)`)  
- Table (quoted):  
  - If subsection contains substantive changes → subsection date =`IRM effective date`.  
  - If changes are editorial but substantive changes exist elsewhere → retain existing subsection date.  
  - If changes are editorial only → date as instructed in `IRM 1.11.2.8.1, Editorial Update Process`.  
  - Incorporates content from issued IPU with no subsequent substantive changes → subsection date =`IPU issue date`.  
  - Incorporates content from issued IPU with subsequent substantive changes → subsection date =`IRM effective date`.  
  - Incorporates content from issued IGM with no subsequent substantive changes → subsection date =`IGM effective date`.  
  - Incorporates content from issued IGM with subsequent substantive changes → subsection date =`IRM effective date`.
